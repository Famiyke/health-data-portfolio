My approach

How I work

Before drawing a conclusion, I check how the data was collected, what the figures mean and whether they answer the question.

Source

Understand the data

I read the original tables and definitions before choosing the measures to analyse.

Prepare

Check the numbers

I look for missing values, check totals and denominators, and make sure the groups can be compared.

Analyse

Work through the analysis

I use Python, SQL and Excel to prepare the data and calculate the results. I keep a record of the steps.

Communicate

Explain the findings

I build charts around the question and explain what the results tell us, including any limitations.
