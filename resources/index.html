Independent data audit

All nine public-data projects were rechecked on 23 November 2026. The review covered original publisher values, denominators, period labels, source references, confidence intervals, forecast and disclosure flags, and key calculations. Browser tests checked 672 dashboard selections, displayed values, status flags, source links, sorting and CSV exports. All nine public-data analyses were rebuilt from the saved original publisher files after the code review. The published values, data extracts and SQL results matched the earlier versions. Each of the seven downloadable workflows now includes a code walkthrough.

  • Canadian health expenditure: 52 annual records and 260 source values. Every displayed value and forecast flag reconciled to CIHI.
  • Nova Scotia diabetes surveillance: 1,288 source strata. Recomputed 115 displayed crude prevalence estimates with their case counts and population denominators.
  • Ontario surgical access: 276 source-table cells inspected. 264 displayed service-area values and headline totals reconciled. The surgery estimation assumption is disclosed.
  • Canadian hospital readmissions: 952 source records independently matched.
  • Canadian mental health outcomes: 248 source records independently matched, including withheld estimates.
  • Manitoba regional health equity: 255 source records independently matched.
  • Canadian workforce and labour market analytics: 3,199 official records independently matched. Published vacancy and overtime rates reconciled to source counts.
  • Canadian retail sales forecasting: 1,806 official records independently matched. 56 model scores and 84 future forecasts independently checked.
  • Canadian federal procurement and supplier analytics: 27,039 included original disclosures matched to the source and 767 dashboard values independently checked.
  • Business process improvement and reporting automation: professional experience figures are self-reported. The aggregate reporting prototype passed ten unit tests, including rejection of extra fields and inconsistent date formats.

Validation results for expenditure, diabetes and surgical access · The six public-data workflow packages include individual validation records. Two Statistics Canada table viewers were unavailable during the link review; the dashboard links use the verified official CSV downloads. The Power BI and Tableau files were inspected structurally; opening them in their native desktop applications remains unverified.
