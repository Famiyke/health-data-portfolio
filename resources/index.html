Independent data audit

All six case studies were rechecked against the downloaded official files on 22 November 2026. The audit covers values, denominators, period labels, source references, forecast and disclosure flags, and key calculations.

  • Canadian health expenditure: 52 annual records and 260 source values. Every displayed value and forecast flag reconciled to CIHI.
  • Nova Scotia diabetes surveillance: 1,288 source strata. Recomputed 115 displayed crude prevalence estimates with their case counts and population denominators.
  • Ontario surgical access: 276 source-table cells inspected. 264 displayed service-area values and headline totals reconciled. The surgery estimation assumption is disclosed.
  • Canadian hospital readmissions: 952 source records independently matched.
  • Canadian mental health outcomes: 248 source records independently matched, including withheld estimates.
  • Manitoba regional health equity: 255 source records independently matched.
